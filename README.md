## 📖 What is RelicsOfCthonia-Drake?

**RelicsOfCthonia-Drake** is a Nether-themed magical expansion for **Slimefun4**. It introduces an abyssal archaeology system, relics categorized across power rarity tiers, transmutation altars, and arcane artifacts granting passive and active perks to Nether explorers.

All content is unlocked and researched directly through the **Slimefun Guide (`/sf guide`)** under the *Relics of Cthonia* category.

---

## ⚔️ Key Features & Relic System

### 💎 1. Relic Rarity Tiers
Relics are acquired via abyssal excavation, ritual altars, and Nether fortress loot chests across distinct power ranks:
* **Common**: *Cthonian Pearl*, *Ring of Suffering*, *Petty Marble Block*, *Healing Draught*, *Petty Mushroom*.
* **Uncommon**: *Pink Salt*, *Skull Hat*, *Cthonian Token*, *Aged Wine*, *Tanzanite Block*, *Blue Glowstone*, *Gray Berry*.
* **Rare**: *Marriage Ring*, *Blaze Ashes*, *Crossed Swords*, *Cerulean Gem*, *Obsidian Eye*.
* **Epic & Legendary**: Arcane transmutation artifacts capable of channeling chthonic essences to forge high-tier combat gear.

### 🏛️ 2. Chthonic Machinery & Ritual Altars
* **Cthonic Altar**: Ritual sacrifice altar to awaken dormant ancient relics.
* **Soul Extractor**: Extracts spiritual essences from abyssal and skeletal remains.
* **Relic Infuser**: Elemental workstation fusing Slimefun alloys with chthonic gems to forge weapons and armor with unique combat traits.

### 🛡️ 3. Combat Perks & Nether Exploration
* Lava resistance, continuous fire immunity within Nether dimensions, and soul-absorption health regeneration.
* Critical damage multipliers against abyssal mobs (Wither Skeletons, Ghasts, Blazes, and Piglins).

## 📥 Installation

1. Download the latest release of `RelicsOfCthonia-drake.jar` from the [Releases](https://github.com/DrakesCraft-Labs/RelicsOfCthonia-drake/releases) page.
2. Place the `.jar` file into your server's `plugins/` directory alongside `Slimefun4-Drake.jar`.
3. Start or restart your server. Recipes will automatically register in `/sf guide`.

---

## 🛠️ Building from Source

```bash
git clone https://github.com/DrakesCraft-Labs/RelicsOfCthonia-drake.git
cd RelicsOfCthonia-drake
mvn clean package
```
